Niseko (ニセコ町)

Welcome to Japan (日本へようこそ)

Skiing

1 hours approx
Brief Itinerary ▼
The rising popularity for ski tourism resulted in a demand for new ski resorts, which led to the opening of Moiwa Ski Resort in 1966, Niseko Annupuri Ski Resort in 1972, and Niseko Higashiyama Ski Resort in 1982. All over Japan and overseas, the three ski resorts are well known for their high-quality powder snow. Skiing in Japan offers powder snow, breathtaking landscapes, and world-class resorts, making it a top destination for winter sports enthusiasts.

Okinawa(沖縄県)

Surfing

1 hours approx
Brief Itinerary ▼
Okinawa, Japan, is a fantastic destination for scuba diving, offering a variety of dive sites and experiences. Popular spots include the Kerama Islands, known for their vibrant coral and sea turtles, and Yonaguni Island, where you can encounter hammerhead sharks and explore the mysterious Yonaguni Monument. Japan scuba diving reveals diverse marine life and vibrant coral reefs, especially in the clear waters around Okinawa and Izu.

Okinawa, Japan, is a popular surf destination known for its warm weather and consistent waves throughout the year. Saga(佐賀県)

Hot Air Ballooning

15 minutes approx
Brief Itinerary ▼
Hot air ballooning in Japan provides stunning panoramic views, with the Saga International Balloon Fiesta being a major attraction for enthusiasts. The Saga International Balloon Fiesta is a major hot air balloon festival held annually in Saga Prefecture, Japan. It is one of Asia's largest hot air balloon events, attracting participants and spectators from around the world. The festival typically takes place in late October to early November along the Kase Riverbed in Saga City.
